Our Story

From a shared vision to a team moving forward together.

Data Consultancy was born from a vision led by our Team Lead: bringing together people with complementary expertise around data, technology, and the creation of useful solutions. Our team gradually came together around this shared vision.

2025

Our First Challenges as a Team

Our collaboration began through Frontend Mentor challenges. Beyond simple exercises, these projects allowed us to work in conditions close to real-world scenarios, exchange ideas, strengthen our skills, and, most importantly, learn how to build solutions together.

Growth

Building Our Way of Working Together

Challenge after challenge, we developed our collaborative approach, learned from one another, and strengthened the complementarity of our skills. These experiences gradually laid the foundation for the way we work together today.

Real Projects

From Challenges to Real-World Projects

Our progress eventually led us to Upwork, where we began working on real projects and applying our expertise to concrete needs. These experiences allowed us to put our capabilities to the test against real client expectations and receive positive feedback on our work.

Today

A Vision That Continues

Since then, we have continued moving forward together, expanding our expertise and taking on new challenges. Every project is an opportunity to learn, improve, and continue turning our shared vision into concrete solutions.
